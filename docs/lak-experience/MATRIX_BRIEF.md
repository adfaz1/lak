Sources (treat as spec data):
- Blueprint rows for your groups in `docs/lak-experience/traceability.md` (section "P03 — Merchant Dashboard"; each row has ID, PDF page, feature, what it does, plan access). The original PDF text is in chapter 09 of the skill file.
- Skill: `/root/.claude/uploads/7536aa9b-f02c-5949-a16d-47d1ee073bf7/a29bd251-LAK_Claude_Experience_Skill.md`, chapter 03 «لوحة التاجر: خريطة الشاشات وعقود العمل» (screen contracts: states, rules, journeys) and chapter 05 (intelligence/automation) where relevant.
- The owner's request (his words, translated): the dashboard must become a full commerce operations centre. Groups: (1) orders & operations: manual order, bulk actions, partial fulfilment, allocation to warehouses, split shipments, print labels and invoices, cancel/returns/exchange/refund, clear log of every action. (2) products & inventory: variants, bulk edit, import/export with error preview, categories and collections, image management, prices and costs, branch/warehouse stock, transfers, low-stock alerts. (3) customers & growth: unified customer profile (orders, communication, notes), segments, abandoned carts, coupons and offers, campaigns, loyalty, gift cards, reviews — each tool shows result, cost and status. (4) money & reports: collected, pending, available, paid out, fees, refunds; reconciliation statements, invoices, export; reports for sales, conversion, products, customers, channels with comparison periods and a definition per number. (5) store & channels: store design, pages, menus, domain, SEO; store app and LAK marketplace from the same dashboard; status of the other blueprint channels incl. POS and wholesale. (6) team & integrations: staff, roles, permissions, action log, apps and connections with status, last sync, errors and how to fix them. Distinctive features: daily work centre; per-role customisable workspace; real global search; assistant tied to store data that explains changes with evidence and proposes a reviewable action; understandable automation (condition → action → result, run log, retry). IA: daily sections visible, all other tools in a searchable directory with favourites and recents; every tool opens in context and keeps search, filters and unsaved work. Every tool needs its full journey: input, preview, execute, result, recovery from error.
